ABOUT HACKVENTURE

HACKVENTURE 3.0 is a collaborative hackathon that brings together students, designers and innovators to build practical technology solutions for real-world Bridging Technology Information and public safety challenges.

Participants will work in teams, explore meaningful problem statements, develop prototypes and present their solutions to an esteemed panel of judges from NIT Mizoram and Mizoram Police.

DAY 1 • 28 OCT 2026

Day 1 – Software Development

Software Development Sprint & AI Analytics

Event Overview

Day 1 features an intensive software development sprint focused on building technology-driven solutions under the theme “Bridging Bytes & Badges”. Participating teams will engineer functional software prototypes, implement AI crime analytics tools, and deploy resilient platforms tailored for law-enforcement agencies and public safety.

Key Objectives

  • Develop practical, high-impact software prototypes addressing published policing challenge statements.
  • Leverage AI models, automated verification pipelines, and data analytics (e.g., ANPR, VAHAN, SARTHI integration).
  • Implement secure, role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption, and offline-first field capabilities.
  • Submit clean, well-documented source code repositories accompanied by a technical architecture summary.

Activities & Competition Details

  • Inauguration & Challenge Briefing (09:00 AM): Official opening ceremony by NIT Mizoram faculty and Mizoram Police leadership.
  • Development Sprint Kickoff (10:30 AM): 24-hour sprint begins with teams designing schemas, APIs, and user interfaces.
  • Mentorship & Expert Checkpoints: Technical checkpoints with senior developers, researchers, and police advisors.
  • Code Freeze & Submission: Repository commit lockdown and submission of source code with documentation.

Rules & Participation Guidelines

  • Open to officially registered teams of 2 to 4 student members.
  • All code must be written during the event; open-source libraries and frameworks may be utilized with proper attribution.
  • Submissions must include repository source code, setup instructions, and a short functional summary.
  • Plagiarism or use of pre-existing commercial products will result in immediate disqualification.
Problem Statements
DAY 2 • 29 OCT 2026

Day 2 – Idea Pitching

Innovation Concepts, Architecture Walkthrough & Jury Presentations

Event Overview

Day 2 highlights Idea Pitching and Prototype Demonstrations. Innovators present strategic concepts, system architecture walkthroughs, and problem-solving solutions directly to a distinguished grand jury panel representing NIT Mizoram academia, Mizoram Police senior leadership, and domain technology veterans.

Key Objectives

  • Articulate problem-solution fit and operational feasibility for state-wide law-enforcement deployment.
  • Demonstrate live working prototypes and interactive software workflows without simulated mockups.
  • Present comprehensive system architectures detailing database scalability, API security, and failover design.
  • Defend engineering decisions, data privacy safeguards, and user experience choices during jury cross-examination.

Activities & Competition Details

  • Jury Briefing & Presentation Sequence (09:30 AM): Orientation and allocation of team presentation slots.
  • Grand Jury Pitch Round: 10-minute presentation followed by a 5-minute technical Q&A defense.
  • Live Prototype Walkthrough: On-stage demonstration of software functionality, mobile UI, and reporting features.
  • Deliberation & Shortlisting: Multi-parameter evaluation across innovation, feasibility, security, and presentation.

Rules & Participation Guidelines

  • All team members must participate in the live pitch and Q&A defense.
  • Slide presentations must adhere to the official hackathon deck guidelines (maximum 10 slides).
  • Prototypes must run live; prerecorded videos may only be used as fallback in the event of hardware connectivity loss.
  • Decisions of the grand jury panel are authoritative, final, and non-negotiable.

DAY 3 • 30 OCT 2026

Day 3 – Quiz

Cybersecurity, Public Safety Laws & Digital Forensics

Event Overview

Day 3 culminates in a high-energy Domain Awareness Quiz testing participant acumen in cybersecurity, public safety laws, digital forensics, IT legislation, and emerging technologies shaping modern law enforcement and citizen protection.

Key Objectives

  • Evaluate foundational and advanced understanding of network security, encryption, and cyber defense protocols.
  • Test knowledge of the Information Technology Act, digital evidence preservation, and public safety regulations.
  • Analyze practical forensic incident scenarios including log analysis, ransomware mitigation, and cyber fraud.
  • Foster domain literacy and technological awareness among tomorrow’s software engineers and civic innovators.

Activities & Competition Details

  • Round 1 – Preliminary Domain Screening (10:00 AM): Written/digital rapid-fire round covering IT laws and cyber fundamentals.
  • Round 2 – Forensic Case Analysis (11:45 AM): Scenario-based round analyzing digital footprints and forensic procedures.
  • Round 3 – Grand Finale Buzzer Round (02:00 PM): Live buzzer championship round on emerging tech, AI ethics, and cyber history.
  • Valedictory & Awards Ceremony (04:00 PM): Felicitation of winners and certificate distribution with dignitaries.

Rules & Participation Guidelines

  • Open to 2-member teams nominated from registered contingents.
  • Electronic gadgets, smartphones, smartwatches, and reference materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Negative marking applies for incorrect answers during the live buzzer stage.
  • The Quiz Master’s decision on answer accuracy and score tabulation is final and undisputed.

PROBLEM STATEMENTS

Problem Goal

Develop a data-capturing and analytics system for monitoring vehicles entering and leaving police check gates.

Key Requirements

  • Digitally record vehicles entering and exiting using ANPR or another suitable method.
  • Acquire and verify vehicle registration details against the VAHAN database.
  • Acquire and verify driver details against the SARTHI database.
  • Generate real-time alerts for suspicious vehicles, including stolen vehicles, vehicles linked to drug trafficking, hit-and-run cases, and other flagged vehicles.
  • Record and update the action taken when a suspicious vehicle is intercepted.
  • Generate analytics showing vehicle movement patterns at check gates.
  • Capture information as quickly as possible to reduce traffic congestion at check gates.

Technology Areas

ANPR Computer Vision AI/ML Database Integration Real-Time Alerts Data Analytics

Problem Goal

Develop an intelligent chatbot to improve police operational efficiency, communication, public engagement, and secure information sharing.

Key Requirements

  • Provide public interaction and timely assistance.
  • Allow citizens to securely share information anonymously while protecting their identity and safety.
  • Support bilingual communication: Mizo — Default, English — Optional.
  • Provide role-based access for authorized police users.
  • Generate a pop-up alert for a predefined authorized person when sensitive information is submitted.
  • Design the application so additional features can be added in the future.

Access Hierarchy

District Level → Range Level → State Level

Technology Areas

AI NLP Multilingual AI Chatbot Role-Based Access Real-Time Alerts Cloud

Problem Goal

Develop a Social Media Monitoring tool for law-enforcement agencies to monitor and analyze social-media activity related to cybercrime and other threats.

Key Requirements

  • Keyword-based search supporting: AND, OR, NOT (Boolean Search).
  • Support phrase searching.
  • Allow collected mentions to be filtered, grouped, marked, and deleted.
  • Support filtering and grouping based on factors such as geography and language.
  • Analyze: Volume, Engagement, Reach, Sentiment, Influence.
  • Use sentiment analysis and daily mention monitoring to help detect potential crisis situations.
  • Measure the volume of specific types of content.
  • Analyze opinions and beliefs expressed in social-media content.
  • Support: English, Mizo.

Technology Areas

AI/ML NLP Sentiment Analysis Social Media Analytics Crisis Detection Cyber Defence

Problem Goal

Build a mobile and web application to make police crime reporting completely paperless.

Current Reports to be Digitized

  • PS Daily Report
  • Arrested Persons Report

Key Requirements

  • Allow reports to be entered digitally or uploaded at the Police Station level.
  • Monitor reporting deadlines and flag non-compliance when reports are not submitted within the specified time.
  • Generate: Daily Reports, Weekly Reports, Monthly Reports.
  • Allow reports to be exported as PDF.
  • Provide search, filtering, and reporting based on selected fields for Police Station data analysis.

Five User Levels & Hierarchical Access

PS → SDPO → SP → DIG → DGP-IGP
  • SDPO: View reports from Police Stations under their jurisdiction.
  • SP: View reports from SDPOs/Police Stations under their jurisdiction.
  • DIG: View reports under SPs within their control.
  • DGP-IGP: View reports from all Police Stations.

Technology Areas

Mobile & Web Role-Based Access Digital Reporting Database Management Data Analytics PDF Reports

Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Submitted Information

The information collected is used exclusively for hackathon organization, team identity verification, communication of schedules and submission guidelines, project evaluation by our judging panel, announcement of winners, and issuance of participation certificates.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards to ensure all participant and project data remains confidential and protected against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse.

4. Sharing of Information

Participant information is shared solely between the co-organizing institutions—National Institute of Technology (NIT) Mizoram and Mizoram Police—and designated hackathon mentors and evaluators for official event coordination. We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal information to third parties.

This website may use essential session cookies and standard anonymous traffic analytics to optimize user navigation and ensure fast, secure page performance. No invasive tracking or commercial profiling cookies are utilized.

Participants may review, update, or request the deletion of their registered contact information at any time by contacting the organizing team at contact@nitmz.ac.in.

HackVenture 3.0 is open to bona fide students currently enrolled in recognized colleges, universities, or technical institutions. All team members must complete official registration before the deadline on 23 October 2026. Teams must adhere to published team size limits.
